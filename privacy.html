Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

yt-autopilot ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channel. By using the app you agree to this Privacy Policy.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

The app asks for two permissions on the signed-in account:

  • Upload YouTube videos (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload).
  • View your YouTube account (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly), used only to read the view, like and comment counts of the videos the app itself uploaded, so it can learn which topics work best.

It does not read comments, subscribers, contacts or any other account data.

What the app stores

  • The sign-in token Google returns, so the app can upload without asking you to sign in every day. It is stored only on your own computer, in a file only your user account can read, and is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The ID of each uploaded video and its view, like and comment counts, in a local history file on your computer. The counts are refreshed when the app runs and the whole history can be deleted at any time. Old video files are deleted from your computer after 14 days.

The app has no server. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

How information is used and shared

The token is used only to upload your videos and read their counts. Nothing is shared with anyone. The app shows no ads and does not let third parties serve content. It does not use cookies or track you.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. You can also delete the secrets folder on your computer to remove the stored token.
