yt-autopilot

A personal tool that helps one creator upload videos to their own YouTube channel.

What it does

yt-autopilot runs on the channel owner's own Mac. Each day it prepares a short video about one AI tool, using information from that tool's official website. The channel owner can watch the video and upload or skip it. If they don't answer before the daily deadline, yt-autopilot uploads it to their own YouTube channel by itself and schedules it to go live.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's account or channel.

YouTube API Services

yt-autopilot uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using yt-autopilot you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
